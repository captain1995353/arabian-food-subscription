✦ Our Story

A Journey Across
Three Continents

Born from a love of bold spices, fragrant rice, and the art of gathering around a table — Arabiana is a culinary celebration where India meets Arabia meets Korea.

Our chefs draw from centuries of culinary tradition — the aromatic depth of Indian curries, the soul-warming richness of Arabian Kabsa, and the addictive crunch of Korean fried chicken — to create something entirely new yet deeply familiar.

Every dish at Arabiana tells a story of heritage, innovation, and the universal language of great food. We believe the best meals are not just eaten — they're experienced.
